# Assumptions

* [low] Generic architecture ranking remains unresolved because the strict same-scale 35B family comparison is blocked on this host. | evidence: with fully matched `archprobe2`/`archprobe2b` settings (`ctx=8192`, `parallel=1`, `q8/q8`, reasoning off), Gemma4-E4B is above Qwen3.5-4B on both `humaneval` and `mbpp`, while Gemma4-E2B is below Qwen3.5-2B on both datasets; a 35B strict q8/q8 family anchor could not be loaded on this 24GB GPU (`cudaMalloc failed: out of memory`). | upgrade needed: lower-quant/offload hardware or a different GPU class.
* [low] Generic KV cache quant coefficients/effects are still not identified across architectures/datasets, despite stronger within-model stability in the current Qwen panel. | evidence: only one architecture family has dense `k/v × ctx × parallel` coverage so far, and a strict 35B continuation OOMed on the current 24GB GPU | upgrade needed: not continuable at current scale; continuation requires lower-quant/offload hardware or a different GPU class.
* [low] EvalPerf is the highest-yield existing generalization benchmark to add next because it is already supported in the harness and adds an efficiency-oriented axis beyond raw correctness. | evidence: evalplus docs and existing EvalPerf support | upgrade needed: not continuable on this host because EvalPerf requires Linux perf permissions (`perf_event_paranoid`) we cannot change here.
* [low] Generic sampling coefficient signs/magnitudes are not identified yet. | evidence: limited sampling diversity on current primary score datasets | upgrade needed: not continuable at this scale without another family/benchmark panel and more sampling diversity.
* [medium] QAT effect likely depends on scale/family and is currently non-uniform: strict E2B matched run (`q8_0/q8_0`, `ctx8192/par1`, greedy) shows large positive deltas, while strict E4B matched run is mixed-sign across datasets. | evidence: E2B strict deltas (`humaneval +0.6280`, `mbpp +0.3466`) vs E4B strict deltas (`humaneval +0.0244`, `mbpp -0.0132`) in current DB | upgrade needed: add at least one more strict matched QAT/non-QAT pair at another size/family before any global QAT claim.
* [medium] Some legacy coefficient instability may come from eval artifact cache collisions in older sweeps. | evidence: JSONL hash collisions across distinct legacy tags were found, including a confirmed cross-family duplicate file pair on disk and multiple same-hash legacy rows in DB-linked artifacts. | upgrade needed: enforce unique sample identity per run and re-run the most collision-prone legacy slices.
* [medium] Per-variable sampling effects are now identifiable on two families but not yet generic. | evidence: `LFM2.5-230M` showed split response for `top_k 40->20` (HE down, MBPP up), while unseen `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Nano-4B` under matched controls shows both datasets down (`HE -0.0061`, `MBPP -0.0317`) with small time increases. | upgrade needed: repeat orthogonal sampling panel on one additional non-LFM family with >1 sampling knob changed one-at-a-time.
* [medium] K- vs V-quant long-context behavior is now contradictory across families and thus still not generic. | evidence: on unseen `granite-4.1-3b` at `ctx24576`, both one-sided settings are non-collapsing and positive (Humaneval/MBPP both above baseline), while prior extreme `Qwen3.6-27B` point showed V-side collapse (`f16/q8_0 -> 0`) on Humaneval. | upgrade needed: add one additional long-context one-sided KV panel on a third family/size anchor to resolve whether collapse is family-specific or artifact-specific.
* [medium] Reasoning effect is still protocol- and family-conditioned, but current fixed-control evidence on stronger anchors now points to score-down/time-up behavior. | evidence: `LFM2.5-230M` had mixed score sign (HE down, MBPP up) with MBPP time up; unseen `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Nano-4B` shows score down on both datasets and large time penalties (`+288.7s`, `+473.3s`). | upgrade needed: run one additional strict on/off reasoning pair on another family anchor with the same fixed settings.
* [medium] Across matched Qwen2B, Qwen4B, and Gemma-E2B panels, local weight-quant movement (`Q2->Q3`) consistently dominates nearby KV perturbation (`q8_0/q8_0 -> q8_0/q5_1`), but larger-scale generalization is still unresolved. | evidence: `manualeig10`, `manualeig12`, and `manualeig13` all show sizable positive `Q2->Q3` score deltas on both datasets, while the KV perturbation stays much smaller and mixed-sign. | upgrade needed: repeat the same ladder on the next larger anchor that fits this host.
* [medium] Gemma-E2B weight-quant response is similarly positive from `Q2_K_XL -> Q3_K_XL`, but comes with large wall-time increases. | evidence: `manualeig12` shows both datasets improve under `q8_0/q8_0` KV, while the KV perturbation at `Q3_K_XL` stays near-neutral/negative. | upgrade needed: repeat on one larger Gemma anchor or one Qwen/Gemma pair with the same q2/q3 ladder to see if the sign holds across scales.
* [medium] Capacity-cap functional form uncertainty is now narrow but not fully resolved between near-equivalent quant caps. | evidence: serial timing-tracked cap search over 27 combinations on current exact-score subset gives best CV RMSE `0.119596` for `(size=rational, act=rational, quant=tanh)` with `(rational,rational,exp)` essentially tied (`0.119604`) and pure rational close (`0.119734`). | upgrade needed: include one larger-scale matched ladder and rerun the same serial search before freezing the exact formula family.
* [medium] Even with expanded chaotic data and deconfounding, pooled global K/V partial effects are still too weak to fix strong architecture-agnostic K/V coefficients. | evidence: orthogonalized residual analysis on 718 unique configs yields `k_ratio` CI crossing zero and `v_ratio` borderline/non-significant. | upgrade needed: targeted decorrelation runs that isolate K and V across at least one additional architecture+context anchor.
* [medium] Quant-ratio direction is now supported in pooled partial-effect space, but exact magnitude stability for formula freezing is not yet guaranteed across future incoming model families. | evidence: orthogonalized estimate on expanded data gives significant positive `quant_ratio` effect with wide CI. | upgrade needed: rerun orthogonalized estimates after next major family influx and compare CI overlap before hard-freezing coefficient priors.
