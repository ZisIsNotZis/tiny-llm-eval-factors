# Resolution / statistical-power analysis

_Generated by `resolution.py`. Every effect estimate in this study must be
read against these floors._

Panel: **1899 runs**, 1142 HumanEval + 757 MBPP.

## Two noise scales, two different questions

| tier | what it bounds | value | how obtained |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 repeat** | same config, same items → is the number reproducible at all? | **0.022** | direct W1 repeat measurement (see below); inferred quasi-replicate sd was 0.0083 (207 cells) |
| **T2 HumanEval item** | does the effect generalise beyond these 164 items? | **0.039** | analytic √(p(1−p)/164), worst case |
| **T2 MBPP item** | same, 378 items | **0.026** | analytic |

**Direct W1 measurement of T1** (`Qwen3.5-2B-UD-Q4_K_XL`, frozen protocol, same 164 items each time):

| decoding | n | scores | sd | range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| humaneval@temp0.0 | 3 | [0.2805, 0.2805, 0.2805] | **0.0** | 0.0 |
| humaneval@temp0.3 | 5 | [0.2256, 0.2378, 0.2622, 0.2561, 0.2378] | **0.0149** | 0.0366 |
| mbpp@temp0.3 | 3 | [0.3466, 0.336, 0.3783] | **0.022** | 0.0423 |

Greedy decoding is **exactly deterministic** (sd = 0.000 across 3 runs) — so for greedy protocols the entire uncertainty is *item sampling* (T2), not run stochasticity. Sampling decoding adds ~0.015, still well below the T2 floor.

A claim about *capability* (the study's stated goal) is limited by **T2**, not T1.
A score difference smaller than the T2 floor is a statement about these particular
problems, not about the model.

→ a **single** matched pair on HumanEval can only resolve |Δ| ≳ **0.108**; on a 44-context aggregate that drops to **0.0163**.

## Why the panel behaves as it does

- HumanEval: median 0.6707, 37% of runs above 0.80 → **ceiling compression**.
- MBPP: median 0.5212, max ~0.80 → less ceiling, but only 378 items and a wide run-to-run spread.
- HumanEval has only **140 distinct score values** in 1142 runs → the metric has ~1/164 resolution.

## Legacy floor correction

The earlier note of an *"irreducible floor ≈ 0.088"* is **unsourced**: no data cell
reproduces it. The two defensible floors are T1 = 0.022 and T2 = 0.039/0.026. Consequence: the earlier figure made the
study look ~10× more precise than the item sets allow.

## Provenance caveats

- DB has **no replicates** (False): the unique index forbids them, so run-to-run stochasticity is **unmeasured** for temp>0 configs.
- Schema is missing: `ctx_size, parallel_slots, start_time, reason, seed, source_file`.
  → every conclusion phrased with `ctx=` / `parallel=` is **unauditable**.
- Exact-score ties across different models are **not** duplication evidence: the
  coarse k/164 grid makes them expected (observed tie-clusters are *below* the null).
