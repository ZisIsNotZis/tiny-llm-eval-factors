# Conclusions / Facts

* Reasoning analysis must use validity decomposition (`P(pass)=P(valid_output)×P(pass|valid_output)`), not raw pass-rate alone. | evidence reference: `report.md` §3.1 and protocol-sweep observations in existing runs | scope/conditions: output protocols where reasoning mode changes output validity behavior.
* Architecture-vs-architecture claims are confounded unless size, activated-size, quant ratio, and decoding protocol are controlled. | evidence reference: `report.md` §3.5 and data adequacy assessment | scope/conditions: current cross-architecture comparisons in present dataset.
* Under the current primary-score protocol, reasoning enabled yields much lower raw pass@1 and much higher wall time than reasoning disabled on both `humaneval` and `mbpp`. | evidence reference: current sqlite aggregates over primary datasets with `reasoning_bool` split | scope/conditions: current prompt/output protocol and decoding settings only.
* Matched-pair reasoning decomposition on primary datasets shows most raw score loss is a validity-collapse effect (`bad_rate_on≈0.80`), while conditional performance is dataset-dependent: mean `Δ(pass|valid)` is negative on HumanEval (`-0.1699`) and slightly positive on MBPP (`+0.0219`). | evidence reference: 171 matched pairs per dataset from `experiments.sqlite` with all non-reasoning factors fixed | scope/conditions: current evaluation protocol, model panel, and existing reasoning on/off runs.
* In the current Qwen3.6-35B KV panel, q8/q5 and q5/q5 consistently outperform q8/q8 on plus pass@1 across Humaneval and MBPP at both `ctx=8192/24576` and `parallel=1/4`, while wall-time differences are small and non-monotonic. | evidence reference: `kvprobe` + `kvprobe2` runs with matched decoding settings | scope/conditions: this model/protocol family only; not yet generalized across architectures.
* Residual `wall_time_sec` nulls are unrecoverable from current artifacts: 42 rows still lack timing, and none of those rows have a direct `.txt` sidecar in `evalplus_results`. | evidence reference: current DB scan plus filesystem check | scope/conditions: current archive state only; future runs can avoid this by emitting timing sidecars consistently.
* Under matched `archprobe2`/`archprobe2b` conditions at `ctx=8192`, `parallel=1`, and `q8/q8`, architecture ordering flips with size in the current Qwen/Gemma pair: Gemma4-E4B beats Qwen3.5-4B on both primary datasets, while Gemma4-E2B trails Qwen3.5-2B on both primary datasets. | evidence reference: current matched-anchor rows in `experiments.sqlite` | scope/conditions: current Qwen/Gemma pair only; does not establish a universal architecture ranking.
* In the current qwen/gemma quant ladders, quant-ratio response is architecture-conditioned and non-monotonic, so a single monotone additive quant term is not adequate for the observed panels. | evidence reference: current qwen/gemma quant ladders and q2/q3 vs q4 reversals | scope/conditions: current observed panels only.
* In the current Qwen3.6-35B setup, KV quantization has a stable score effect (`q8/q5` and `q5/q5` above `q8/q8`) across `ctx=8192/24576` and `parallel=1/4`, while wall-time impact is near-neutral and sign-inconsistent. | evidence reference: `kvprobe` + `kvprobe2` contrasts on Humaneval/MBPP with matched decoding settings | scope/conditions: this model/protocol family only.
* In the current MTP timing probes, MTP mainly affects wall-time and has no stable direct quality gain. | evidence reference: current timing probes plus the existing MTP rows on HumanEval | scope/conditions: current sampled protocol and datasets only.
* In the current Qwen sampling panel, `top_k` is the dominant lever while temperature has weak effect at fixed `top_k`: HumanEval stays at baseline for `k=20` (`t=0.3/0.6`) but drops for `k=64`, while MBPP improves at `k=64` and is lower at `k=20` almost independent of temperature. | evidence reference: 2x2 panel (`t∈{0.3,0.6}`, `k∈{20,64}`) on Humaneval/MBPP plus scores | scope/conditions: current Qwen panel only.
* Full-data structural formula refit on all 1749 deduplicated rows (25 families, 181 checkpoints) with GroupKFold-by-checkpoint CV achieves RMSE 0.1952 (checkpoint-held-out), 0.2942 (family-held-out), 0.1935 (random). Beats ridge baseline (0.2097 checkpoint-held-out). Irreducible floor from unrecorded context/stochastic noise within identical-factor cells is ~0.088. | evidence reference: `refit_results.json` from `refit_formula.py` full-data run | scope/conditions: pooled fit across all available data; family-held-out (0.294) is harder because family weights absorb size/quant effects.
* The canonical formula structure: `pred = 1 - exp(-raw)`, `raw = exp(linear) × cap_size(size_b) × cap_act(act_ratio) × cap_quant(quant_ratio) × (1 + a_qat×qat)`, `linear = w[family] + d[dataset] + br×reason_off + bt×temp + btopk×log(top_k) + bkvk×k_ratio + bkvv×v_ratio`. Caps: size=rational (x/(x+k)), act=tanh(k×x), quant=rational (x/(x+k)). Guarantees `score→0` when size_b, act_ratio, or quant_ratio → 0. | evidence reference: `score_model.py` generated from `refit_results.json` | scope/conditions: current formula family; the tanh(26.8×x) cap for act_ratio saturates at ~1 for all observed ratios, making activated-size effectively inert in the pooled fit.
* A structural-prior constrained sigmoid model (monotone size/activation, nonnegative reasoning, numeric quant ratio, and `activated_size->0 => score->0`) is incompatible on the tested snapshot: RMSE rises from `0.1168` (unconstrained same feature set) to `0.3786`, with reasoning and quant-ratio effects forced to boundary (`≈0`). | evidence reference: constrained optimization on legacy usable snapshot (`n=542`) | scope/conditions: tested snapshot indicates data/model mismatch; not a universal proof.
* In the long-context one-sided KV split on `Qwen3.6-27B-UD-IQ2_M` at `ctx=24576` (Humaneval), K-only quantization (`k=q8_0,v=f16`) keeps `pass@1_plus=0.00610` while V-only quantization (`k=f16,v=q8_0`) drops to `0.00000`, suggesting stronger V-side degradation at this extreme setting. | evidence reference: `kvlongctx-konly` vs `kvlongctx-vonly` rows in `experiments.sqlite` | scope/conditions: single model/dataset/extreme-context point; low generality.
* QAT evidence is now mixed-sign in strict E4B matching (`q8/q8`, `ctx8192/par1`, reasoning off): Humaneval improves (`0.8049 -> 0.8293`) while MBPP declines (`0.6667 -> 0.6534`), so a single global QAT uplift is not supported yet. | evidence reference: `gemma-4-E4B-it-UD-Q4_K_XL` vs `gemma-4-E4B-it-qat-UD-Q4_K_XL` anchors | scope/conditions: current E4B panel only.
* A hash-based artifact audit found cache-collision style duplicates in legacy eval outputs (including one confirmed cross-family duplicate file pair), so those affected slices are lower-confidence for coefficient interpretation. | evidence reference: JSONL SHA256 collision scan over evalplus artifacts and DB-linked source files | scope/conditions: current artifact archive only.
* Strict E2B QAT-vs-nonQAT matching at `q8_0/q8_0`, `ctx8192`, `parallel=1`, reasoning off shows large positive deltas on both primary datasets (`humaneval: 0.0793 -> 0.7073`, `mbpp: 0.1852 -> 0.5317`). | evidence reference: `gemma-4-E2B-it-qat-UD-Q4_K_XL-manualeig2-*` vs non-QAT E2B strict anchors in `experiments.sqlite` | scope/conditions: current E2B panel only; does not imply global QAT uplift.

* **Quant attribution caveat (honest finding)**: The pooled formula gives a weak quant effect (k_quant=5.54 rational cap → quant_ratio 0.35→0.66 produces cap range 0.059→0.106), but matched local ladders show large Q2→Q3 deltas (+0.07 to +0.17 in score). This is because weight-quant is between-checkpoint (each gguf file has one quant), and family weights + size absorb the quant effect. The formula's quant attribution is unreliable; the causal quant effect should be read from matched local ladders (conclusions #26, #28, #31, #34). | evidence reference: pooled refit vs matched local contrasts in `experiments.sqlite` | scope/conditions: structural limitation of cross-sectional data; within-family quant ladders are the correct source for quant effect size.
* **Activated-size caveat**: k_act=26.8 (tanh) makes the act_ratio cap ~1.0 for all observed ratios, so activated-size is effectively inert in the pooled fit. This contradicts the prior that activated-size should matter. Possible causes: (a) activated-size variance is low in the dataset (most models have act_ratio=1), (b) activated-size effect is absorbed by the family weight, (c) activated-size genuinely doesn't matter for these benchmarks at this scale. | evidence reference: refit results; models with activated_size < size are rare in the dataset | scope/conditions: pooled fit; targeted within-family activated-size ablation would resolve this.
