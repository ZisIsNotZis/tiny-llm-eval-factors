> Two consequences:
> 1. **The flagship "reasoning collapses output validity" result is falsified as stated.** Every
>    `bad_rate >= 0.5` row but one (188/189) comes from the chaotic random-sampling sweep
>    (`top_k` up to 2048, random `temperature`/`top_p`). Under clean fixed decoding, `auto` vs
>    `off` bad_rate differs by only 0.02–0.04. Validity collapse is a *sampling-parameter* effect,
>    not a reasoning effect. (The score effect of reasoning is still real and large — see below.)

>    MDE is ~0.084; only 4 of 36 claims clear it. Treat un-marked entries as hypotheses, not facts.
> 3. Each conclusion also inherits the schema gap: `ctx_size`, `parallel_slots`, `start_time`,
>    `reason`, `seed` are **not stored**, so any `ctx=`/`parallel=` scope is unauditable.
